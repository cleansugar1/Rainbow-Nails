About us

Proudly serving the island of Oʻahu with high-quality nail and personal care services for over 20 years.

Two decades in Kaimukī

Rainbow Nails has been part of the Kaimukī neighborhood for more than 20 years. Over that time we’ve done first manicures for keiki, nails for big days, and standing appointments for regulars who’ve become friends.

We keep things simple: clean tools, careful work, and nails done the way you asked for them. Whether it’s a polish change on your lunch break or a Volcano Detox pedicure on a day off, you’ll get the same attention.

Find us on Koko Head Avenue, with plenty of parking out back.

  • Cut to your needs

    Tell us how short, how square, how sparkly. We listen first.

  • Comfortable chairs

    Our pedicure chairs are made for settling in and relaxing.

  • Something for everyone

    Nails, lashes, waxing, facials, and services for kids 9 and under.
