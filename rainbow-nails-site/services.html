Services

Nails, lashes, waxing and facials. Call us to book or ask about anything you don’t see here.

Manicures

  • Regular manicure

    Nail shaping, cuticle care, buffing, cuticle oil, massage, hot towel and a color of your choice.

  • Deluxe manicure

    Everything in our regular manicure, plus a paraffin wax treatment.

Pedicures

  • Spa pedicure

    Nail shaping, cuticle care, buffing, cuticle oil, sea salt scrub, massage, hot towel and a color of your choice.

  • Deluxe spa pedicure

    Our spa pedicure plus callus removal and a paraffin wax treatment.

  • Organic Rainbow pedicure

    Callus removal, collagen cream mask, collagen massage lotion, paraffin wax, sea salt scrub, massage and color.

  • Volcano Detox pedicure

    Our most indulgent: detox volcano crystals, sugar scrub, collagen mask and lotion, and a longer massage.

  • Mani & pedi combos

    Pair any manicure with any pedicure, from regular to Volcano Deluxe.

Gel, acrylic & dip

  • Gelish gel polish

    Gel manicures and pedicures, including deluxe, organic and Volcano gel pedicures.

  • Acrylic

    New sets and fills with regular or gel polish.

  • Gel-X & hard gel

    Gel-X extensions and hard gel on natural nails or with tips.

  • Dipping powder

    Dip on natural nails or with tips.

  • Polish change

    A fresh coat of regular or gel polish on hands or toes.

  • Soak-off

Nail art & extras

Lashes

Waxing & facials
